HelpMum Africa Foundation (“HelpMum”, “we”) is responsible for your information. Your pregnancy and health information is sensitive, and we protect it in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

## WHAT WE COLLECT

The details you give us when you sign up, and the questions and information you share with MamaBot AI.

## WHY WE USE IT

To answer your questions, run and secure the app, support you, and improve MamaBot AI’s safety and accuracy. We use anonymised data for improvement wherever possible.

## HOW WE PROTECT IT

Your information is stored securely and encrypted. Only authorised staff who are bound by confidentiality can access it.

## WHO SEES IT

We do not sell your information. We share only anonymised data with partners, who may not try to identify you. We disclose personal information only where the law requires it or to protect life.

## YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, withdraw your consent, or object to how we use it. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT

You can delete your account in the app. You have 90 days to recover it by emailing info@helpmum.org. After 90 days, your account and history are permanently deleted.

## PLEASE REMEMBER

- Share only what you are comfortable sharing.

- Do not include other people’s personal details in your questions.

- MamaBot AI is not a doctor and cannot handle emergencies.
