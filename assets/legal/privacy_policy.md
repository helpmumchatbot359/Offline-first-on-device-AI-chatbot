# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 20, 2026

Welcome! This Privacy Policy explains how HelpMum Foundation ("HelpMum", "we", "us", and "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data from our website visitors, users of our web and mobile apps/software, and representatives of businesses that use our mobile apps/software ("Users"). It also provides details on how to contact us with privacy-related questions. The provisions contained in this Privacy Policy supersede all notices and statements regarding our privacy practices with respect to our services.

## What does this Privacy Policy cover?

This policy applies to all of HelpMum's activities that involve handling personal data, including our website and all related websites and apps (collectively, the "Website") and services (collectively, the "Services"). By using our Services on any device (desktop, laptop, mobile phone, tablet, etc.), you agree to this Privacy Policy. This policy also covers offline interactions with us.

## What Information do We Collect and How Do We Collect It?

We collect personal information in various ways depending on how you interact with us and use our Services.

### a) Information You Provide

You may provide personal information when you:

- Sign up or register on our web or mobile apps.
- Fill out forms online or offline.
- Share Know-Your-Customer information and documentation.
- Enter contracts with us or register for our events.
- Interact with us on social media or communicate via phone, email, or other means.

This information may include:

- Identity and Profile Information: First name, last name, maiden name, password, title, date of birth, state of origin, nationality, and gender.
- Contact Information: Delivery address, email address, and telephone numbers.
- Device Information: Details about the devices you use to access our Services.
- Usage Information: Details of your use of our Services.
- Marketing and Communications Information: Your preferences for receiving marketing communications.
- Location Information: Your current location as disclosed by GPS or other technology.

We may also collect health information related to our services. We do not collect sensitive personal data like sexual orientation, political opinions, or criminal records.

### b) Information Automatically Collected

We automatically collect information about your use of our Services, which may include:

- Device Information: Hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
- Log Information: IP address, device identification numbers, device events (like crashes), system activity, and hardware settings.

### c) Information Collected Through Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies to personalize our Services and gather aggregate information about usage. Cookies are text files or other identifiers placed by your browser. We use:

- Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser.
- Persistent Cookies: Cookies that remain until they expire or are deleted.
- Third-Party Cookies: Cookies placed by third parties to provide insights into service usage and demographics.

You can control how your browser handles cookies via its privacy settings. Refusing cookies may limit some features of our Services.

## What Other Privacy Rights Do You Have?

Beyond your right to opt out of our processing Personal Information, you also have the option of exercising any of the rights below with respect to your Personal Information:

### Request Correction of Your Personal Information

We respect your need to correct any inaccurate information. Please inform us of any changes to your personal information by contacting us at the details provided at the end of this Privacy Policy. Note that any changes you request will only affect future use of your information, and we may need to verify the accuracy of the new data you provide.

### Request Access to Your Personal Information

You have the right to know if we are storing your personal information and can request a copy of it. Before we provide any personal information, we will ask you to verify your identity. If you cannot verify your identity, we reserve the right to deny your request. We aim to respond to your requests for information promptly.

### Request Restrictions on Data Processing

You may object to how we process your personal information if you believe it impacts your fundamental rights and freedoms. You also have the right to object to our processing of your information for direct marketing purposes. In some cases, we may demonstrate that we have compelling legitimate grounds to continue processing your information.

### Request Deletion of Your Personal Information

You can ask us to delete or remove your personal information if there is no legal basis for us to continue processing it. This right also applies if you successfully object to our processing, if we have processed your information unlawfully, or if we must erase your information to comply with legal requirements. Please note that we may not always be able to comply with your deletion request for specific legal reasons, which we will communicate to you at the time of your request.

### Request Data Transfer

You can request that we transfer your personal information to you or a third party in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format. This right only applies to automated information which you initially consented for us to use or where we used the information to perform a contract with you.

### Withdraw Consent

If we rely on your consent to process your personal information, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. However, this will not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before you withdraw your consent. If you withdraw your consent, we may not be able to provide certain services to you. We will inform you if this is the case at the time you withdraw your consent.

### Object to Automated Decision-Making

You have the right to object to decisions made by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects on you, subject to applicable law.

## How Do We Secure and Store Your Information?

We have implemented strict physical, electronic, and administrative security measures to prevent your personal information from being accidentally lost, misused, or accessed by unauthorized individuals. Access to your personal data is restricted to employees, agents, contractors, and third parties who need to know the information for business purposes. They will only process your personal information following our instructions. In case of a suspected data breach, we have procedures in place to respond promptly and will notify you and the relevant regulators as required by law. Our third-party service providers must also maintain a comprehensive information security program, including administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information from loss, misuse, or alteration. While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You also play a crucial role in protecting your information; please ensure you safeguard it and avoid sharing it with others.

## How Long Do We Retain Your Personal Information?

Subject to applicable law, which might, from time to time, oblige us to store your Personal Information for a certain period, we will retain your Personal Information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes we collected it for, including the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

## How Do We Protect Children's Privacy?

We respect the privacy of children and we do not knowingly collect Personal Information from children under the age of 18. If you are under the age of eighteen (18), do not use our Services or submit any information to us.

While delivering our services, we may use third-party servers and databases located in other countries, which means your personal data might be transferred to places with different data protection laws than Nigeria. When we transfer personal data outside Nigeria, we take steps to ensure it is secure and handled according to relevant data protection laws. If we need to transfer your personal information to a country with insufficient data protection laws, we will take all necessary steps to obtain your informed consent and make you aware of any associated risks. In all cases, we will ensure your personal data is transmitted safely and securely.
